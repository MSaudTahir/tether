# Notes for AI assistants

Follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) (architecture and rules) and check
[ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) (progress and plans) before starting. In particular:

- Update README.md and ROADMAP.md in the same change as the work they describe.
- Never commit secrets or personal details (keys, passwords, machine names, addresses, emails).
- Ask before changing settings, apps or files on a real phone, and before moving or deleting tags
  or anything else that rewrites published history.
